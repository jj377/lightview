Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

1. What we collect

  • Account data — your name, email address, and password (stored as a secure hash) when you sign up.
  • Billing data — handled entirely by our Merchant of Record, Creem. We never see or store your full payment card details.
  • Product data — the data sources you connect (files, sheets, databases) and the dashboards you create, stored to provide the service.
  • Usage data — basic analytics about how the app is used, to improve it.

2. How we use data

We use your data only to operate, secure, and improve LightView — for example, rendering your dashboards, billing through Creem, and responding to support requests. We do not sell your data or use your business data for advertising.

3. Sharing

We share data only with the processors needed to run the service: our hosting provider, our database provider, and Creem (payments/tax). We may disclose data if required by law.

4. Retention and deletion

You can delete your data sources and dashboards at any time from within the app. You can request full account deletion at any time; we remove your personal data within 30 days, except records we must keep for billing/tax compliance.

5. Security

We use encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS) everywhere, store passwords hashed, and keep access to production data restricted to the minimum needed.

6. Your rights

Depending on your jurisdiction (e.g. EU/EEA under GDPR), you may have rights to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data. Contact us and we will respond within 30 days.

7. Contact

Privacy questions? Reach us via GitHub issues.
